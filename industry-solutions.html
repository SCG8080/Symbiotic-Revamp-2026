Financial Services

Emerging technologies continue to drive tremendous structural change in financial services institutions. The need to comply with regulations is accelerating change in the market, driving requirements for system capacity, data, volume, and more intelligent security. Consumers' demand for immediacy and the latest technology, including mobile and wireless devices, contactless payments, and service-oriented architecture (SOA), are causing financial institutions to rethink customer interfaces, delivery channels, information security, and business process agility. Financial organizations are actively pursuing avenues that will streamline, optimize, and automate their IT architectures, products, and customer services. Symbiotic Consulting Group has the financial services expertise to develop solutions that help companies meet these objectives.

Healthcare

Economic pressures of global competition are forcing employers to shift healthcare costs to their employees. This is giving rise to consumer directed healthcare and healthcare savings accounts, generating a profound ripple effect in the healthcare ecosystem. As consumers are paying more out-of-pocket for healthcare services, and as information becomes more widely accessible, medical service providers can now compete based on price, convenience, high tech, and high touch. Symbiotic Consulting Group can help you healthcare organization stay competitive by developing a solution that streamlines your administrative processes, better manages patient information, and provides convenient and secure access to critical applications, while improving efficiency, productivity, and responsiveness.

Distribution / Supply Chain

As a distribution/supply chain service provider, your customers represent many industries with varying requirements. Managing multiple warehouses, inventories, clients and channels means visibility into your operations is vital. Symbiotic Consulting Group can offer your organization the comprehensive functionality you need to accommodate a wide range of distribution requirements across vertical markets.


Our Distribution/Supply Chain Management Solutions provide the flexibility and speed you need to handle demanding clients, high volumes of small orders, value-added services, and quick delivery timeframes. Our solutions support a demand-driven supply chain so you can adapt your operations as volume levels, delivery schedules and customer requirements change.

Legal Services

Law firms are seeking innovative solutions and technology that will enable them to improve client service, satisfaction, and retention. At the same time, they are looking for new ways to increase profitability by controlling costs and making their business more efficient. Customer expectations for the legal profession are changing as well, as clients demand new services and more immediate access to attorneys and information. To overcome these challenges, Symbiotic Consulting Group has developed innovative solutions and applications that can enable law firms to deliver better client service, differentiate themselves from competitors, and control costs. In conjunction with these applications, we also have the networking capabilities to ensure secure, reliable systems that can help them build client loyalty and deliver a high return on investment.
